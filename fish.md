## What I do

I work on applied AI reliability: retrieval grounded in sources, refusal when evidence is missing, deterministic checks, approval gates, and evaluations. The goal is to make failure visible and give people control over consequential actions.

I am based in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil (GMT-3), and open to remote applied-AI roles and fixed-price freelance work. For current availability and terms, ask me directly.
