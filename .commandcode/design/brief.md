# Design brief — Vinícius Raposo portfolio

**Name:** Vinícius Raposo — Applied AI Reliability Engineer
**Category:** Personal portfolio and proof dossier for applied-AI reliability.
**User:** A recruiter, hiring manager, or founder under time pressure, checking whether the claims are backed by public evidence.
**Job:** Decide quickly whether to open a project, contact Vinícius, or hire him.
**Artifact:** Seven public proof systems, their tests, evidence hashes, live demos, and stated limits.
**Evidence:** Test counts, checksums, live demos, source links, verification records, and explicit boundaries.
**Drift to refuse:** dark SaaS/orange template, stat monument, AI-indigo, generic feature-card grid, icon toppers, gradient text, claims without evidence.
**Register:** Brand. The interface is the portfolio; the proof is the product.
